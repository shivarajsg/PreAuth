================================================================================
SYSTEM OVERVIEW
================================================================================
MedRule is a deterministic clinical decision support (CDS) system for prior 
authorization. It operates with 0% LLM or probabilistic inference, using 
three-valued boolean logic (TRUE, FALSE, UNKNOWN) across 372 structured rules 
in 9 clinical domains. It strictly enforces a zero-approved safety invariant 
and unconditional non-binding decision status (is_binding: false).

================================================================================
STEP-BY-STEP WALKTHROUGH FOR THE REVIEWER
================================================================================

1. System Dashboard & Governance Invariants
-------------------------------------------
URL: https://medrule-preauth.onrender.com/dashboard

What to observe:
* Rule Knowledge Base: Total 372 rules (322 in DRAFT, 50 in NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW, 
  and strictly 0 in APPROVED).
* Safety Invariant: The system visually enforces that no rule can be auto-approved; 
  human clinical sign-offs are mandatory.
* Role Switcher (Top Right): Switch personas to test Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):
  - Dr. Sarah Chen, MD (Clinical SME Reviewer)
  - Dr. Eleanor Vance, MD (Medical Director)
  - Quality Auditor (Read-Only Viewer)


2. Rule Explorer & Clinical Policy Workbench
--------------------------------------------
URL: https://medrule-preauth.onrender.com/rules

What to test:
* Use the Domain Filter to filter by clinical domains (e.g., Cardiology, 
  Radiology, Oncology, Musculoskeletal).
* Search by code or condition (e.g., type "70450" for CT Head or "93306" for 
  Echocardiography).
* Click on any rule to view its Rule Workbench:
  - Structured Logic: View deterministic boolean logic trees (AND/OR condition groups).
  - Source Provenance: View the exact verbatim quotation from the clinical guideline 
    policy along with physical page citations.


3. Human Review Queue & Governance Workflow
-------------------------------------------
URL: https://medrule-preauth.onrender.com/queue

What to test:
* View the 50 rules flagged as NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW due to ambiguous clinical 
  criteria or edge cases.
* Test submitting a review note or proposing a criteria correction.
* Check the 8 mandatory approval prerequisites (requires dual clinician review, 
  citation validation, and medical director authorization before a rule can ever 
  be promoted).


4. Case Intake & Live Deterministic Evaluation
----------------------------------------------
URL: https://medrule-preauth.onrender.com/intake

What to test:
* Click the "Load Sample Case" button to populate a realistic synthetic 
  prior-authorization request (or use one of the Quick Clinical Presets).
* Click "Run Intake & Evaluate".
* Observe the 10-Stage Pipeline in Action:
  1. Member eligibility & benefit verification (simulated connector).
  2. Prior-authorization requirement check.
  3. Policy routing to the exact clinical guideline.
  4. Deterministic condition evaluation (MET, NOT_MET, or UNKNOWN).
  5. Final assessment summary with full citation trace and non-binding disclaimer.


5. Pilot Adjudication & Concordance Tracking
--------------------------------------------
URL: https://medrule-preauth.onrender.com/pilot

What to test:
* Review pilot cohort evaluations comparing deterministic machine assessments 
  with documented human adjudication.
* Inspect root-cause discrepancy categories (e.g., documentation ambiguity, 
  threshold mismatch, or missing clinical notes).


6. System Health & Compliance Verification
------------------------------------------
URL: https://medrule-preauth.onrender.com/api/health

What to observe:
* A machine-readable JSON status verifying:
  - Database connectivity: CONNECTED
  - Active mock connectors: MOCK (no PHI transmitted)
  - Outbound submission adapter: DISABLED (permanently locked)
  - Enforced zero-approved safety invariants: TRUE
  - Evaluator binding status: is_binding: false
