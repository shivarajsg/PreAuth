# Interventional Pain Management — Clinical Rules & Decision Logic (Phase 2 Placeholder)

> [!NOTE]
> This directory is reserved for validated machine-readable criteria, decision trees, and deterministic rule logic to be extracted during **Phase 2**.

## Phase Separation Principles

1. **Policy Document (`policy_pdfs/`)**: Official clinical guidelines and place-of-service policies establishing coverage criteria and medical necessity requirements.
2. **Supporting Document (`supporting_pdfs/`)**: Workflow aids, prior-authorization checklists, clinical history forms, and provider tip sheets.
3. **Referenced Source (`referenced_sources/`)**: Authoritative external citations, CMS manuals, LCDs, NCDs, and regulatory provisions.
4. **Rule (`rules/`)**: Individual criteria, logical conditions, thresholds, exceptions, and exclusions.

## Phase 2 Scope

Structured deterministic rules (e.g. JSON schemas, criteria graphs, eligibility evaluation routines) will be systematically derived from the verified 2026 governing policy documents in Phase 2. No unverified or synthetic rule records have been placed here in Phase 1.5.
