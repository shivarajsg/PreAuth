# Domain Summary: Radiation Oncology

## 1. Domain Scope and Clinical Coverage
Covers external beam radiotherapy (EBRT, 3D-CRT, IMRT), stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS/SRT), stereotactic body radiation therapy (SBRT/SABR), proton beam therapy (PBT), neutron beam therapy, brachytherapy, and radiopharmaceuticals across malignant and benign indications.

## 6. Items Requiring Plan-Specific or Payer-Specific Access
- **Health-Plan Specific Prior Authorization Grids:** Certain individual commercial health plans (e.g. state BlueCross BlueShield plans, regional Medicare Advantage plans) establish custom procedure pre-authorization lists or carve-outs. These matrices are proprietary to individual health plan contracts and require contracted provider portal credentials.
- **State Medicaid Administrative Fee Schedules:** State Medicaid programs (e.g. Texas HHSC, Florida AHCA) may impose statutory visit caps, prior authorization exemption thresholds, or state-specific billing modifier requirements that are not published in national clinical guidelines.
- **Access Limitation:** These documents require health-plan contract logins or state agency portals and are not published on the public RadMD / Evolent clinical guidelines site.
